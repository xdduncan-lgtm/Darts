DARTS SCOREBOARD FOR iPHONE (works offline)
===========================================
One-time setup on your Windows computer, about 5 minutes:

1. Unzip this download. You'll have a folder named "Darts-Scoreboard".
2. On github.com click "+" > New repository. Name it: darts. Public. Create.
3. Click "uploading an existing file", drag in EVERYTHING inside the
   Darts-Scoreboard folder, and click Commit changes.
4. In the repository click Settings > Pages. Under Branch choose "main" and
   "/ (root)", then Save. Wait 1-2 minutes.
5. Your app is at:  https://YOUR-USERNAME.github.io/darts/

On your iPhone:
6. Open that link in Safari. Tap the Share button > Add to Home Screen > Add.
7. Open it once from the Home Screen while online. After that it works with no
   internet, full-screen, with its own darts icon.

Games in progress are saved on the phone. To update the app later, upload the
new index.html over the old one and change darts-v1 to darts-v2 in sw.js.
Flag, animal and icon images: Twemoji by Twitter/X and contributors, CC-BY 4.0.
